Methodology and data
How the numbers are made
The portfolio has 180 loans across five regions and five products. Each loan is simulated month by month from its disbursal:
interest on the opening balance at rate ÷ 1200, then the payment. Before the 12-month window every installment is paid in full;
inside it, Overdue and NPA loans pay several partial installments, and Closed loans pay a few partials and then settle the
balance.
• EMI: the standard amortising installment, P·i·(1+i)n ÷ ((1+i)n − 1), rounded to the paisa.
• Collection efficiency: amount collected ÷ amount due in the month. Settlements of Closed loans count as collections, so a
month can exceed 100%.
• Overdue: days past due above 30. NPA: over 180 days past due. Ratios are shares of outstanding.
• DPD buckets: 0, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+.
Data is synthetic (seed 20261004). Parameters filter the KPIs, the pivot, the status and DPD charts and the register; the monthly
series and the drill-through pages always show the whole book.
