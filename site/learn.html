● Recipes · made by hand in the designer

The hard reports, click by click.

Each recipe below was built in the ReportWright designer by clicking and typing, with no hand-written report JSON. A Playwright script drives the same designer you can open on this page, takes the screenshots, and saves the finished .pw.json; the numbered steps are the steps that script performs. Open any result in the playground to take it apart.
